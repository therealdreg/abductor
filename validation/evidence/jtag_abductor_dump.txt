FULL JTAG (not just SWD) of the CW312T-SAM4S THROUGH the Abductor on the CW308 UFO.
Chain: Husky Plus MPSSE -> CW308 UFO -> Abductor -> CW312T-SAM4S.
This adds the two remaining debug data lines JTAG_TDI and JTAG_TDO on top of the SWD result,
so it exercises MOSI and MISO as well.

Non-DIO MPSSE (enable_MPSSE(1)); the Husky drives JTAG on the main 20-pin SPI/PDI pins.
Bridge on the CW308 UFO (6 wires): SWD 4 wires + 2 more for TDI/TDO:
  UFO SCK   -> J8 J_TCK   (JTAG_TCK)
  UFO PDID  -> J8 J_TMS   (JTAG_TMS)
  UFO MOSI  -> J8 J_TDI   (JTAG_TDI)   <- added for full JTAG
  UFO MISO  -> J8 J_TDO   (JTAG_TDO)   <- added for full JTAG
  UFO nRST  -> J8 nRST    (JTAG_nRST)
  UFO VREF  -> J8 J_UREF  (JTAG_VREF)
